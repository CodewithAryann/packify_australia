Makeup Boxes: Cosmetic Display and Gift Packaging

Designing Makeup Boxes Around Palettes and Shade Stories

Makeup lines built around shade stories (neutrals, brights, seasonal edits, influencer collaborations) need packaging that showcases the range clearly from the front panel while protecting fragile pans inside.Custom makeup boxes are structured for the exact footprint and thickness of your palette or compact, with options for raised platforms, foam wells, or trays that keep powders intact through international shipping and retail handling.

Finishes and Windows That Sell at the Tester Wall

In cosmetics aisles, customers scan rows of products quickly. Window cutouts combined with finishes like rose gold foil, holographic accents, or soft-touch matte help your boxes stand out in a crowded tester bay.We help you place windows so pan layouts show clearly without sacrificing structural strength, and select finish combinations that photograph well for social and e-commerce.

  • Strategically placed windows that frame shade ranges without weakening the carton
  • Soft-touch or gloss finishes tuned to your brand’s aesthetic (clinical, glam, indie, etc.)
  • Interior printing that extends palette artwork into the full unboxing experience
