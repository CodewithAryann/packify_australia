Custom Cosmetic Boxes | Beauty Packaging Across All Three Structures

Cosmetic packaging is rarely one structure. A single beauty line typically uses tuck boxes for retail-priced SKUs, rigid boxes for hero products and gift sets, and mailers for D2C shipping. The challenge is keeping the brand consistent across all three. Tuck boxes: Lipstick, compacts, palettes, mascara, lip balm. Lower unit cost, fast assembly, large flat panels for full-bleed print. Rigid boxes: Hero products, limited editions, gift sets, fragrance launches. 1200–1800 GSM chipboard wrapped in art paper, linen, or fabric. Higher unit cost, premium presentation. Mailer boxes: Subscription boxes, full routine D2C shipping. Self-locking flaps, interior print for unboxing reveals, sized to fit subscription kit contents. Finishes (across all three structures): Rose gold and gold foil, holographic, soft-touch matte, gloss, glitter lamination, spot UV. Compliance: Side and back panels reserved for INCI lists, free-from claims, manufacturer information, multilingual copy.

Custom Cosmetic Boxes | Tuck, Rigid & Mailer Formats for Beauty SKUs

Cosmetic packaging is rarely one structure. A single beauty line can include lip balm sticks, glass cream jars, palettes, and full gift sets. Each needs a different carton. Tuck boxes for retail compacts and lipstick. Rigid boxes for hero products and gift sets. Mailers for D2C shipping. The structure decides cost, presentation, and how the box performs at retail or in transit.

Three Structures, One Brand System

Tuck for compacts, palettes, lipstick. Rigid for limited editions, hero products, gift sets. Mailer for shipping kits and subscription boxes. We help you map each SKU to the right format so the line looks consistent without over-spending on structure where it does not pay back.

Compliance Panel Zones

INCI lists, SPF values, free-from claims, manufacturer information, and multilingual copy all need legible space. Layouts reserve side and back panels for compliance text so the front face stays branded.

Window Cutouts for Shade Visibility

Retail buyers want to see shade before they pick up the product. Window placement is calibrated so it shows the relevant part of the product (powder pan, lipstick bullet, serum color) without weakening the carton.

Beauty-Category Finishes

Rose gold foil, holographic accents, soft-touch matte, glitter lamination, spot UV. The finishes that photograph well for social and e-commerce while still looking elevated in person.

Pantone Shade Family Coordination

External carton colors Pantone-matched to internal shade families. Buyers can find the exact shade in a 30-shade lipstick line without reading a single label.

Inserts for Multi-Product Sets

EVA foam, paper trays, magnetic flaps, ribbon pulls. Built around the product layout so gift sets feel curated, not crammed.

Cross-Format Brand Consistency

When a single line uses tuck, rigid, and mailer structures, we keep print, color systems, and panel hierarchies consistent so the family reads as one brand across all three formats.

Window Placement Engineering

Cutout shape, position, and surrounding carton thickness calibrated to show the relevant product part (powder pan, lipstick bullet, serum color) without weakening the carton or breaking the flat-pack ship profile.

Pantone Shade Family Coordination

External carton colors matched to internal shade families. Buyers can navigate a 30-shade lipstick line by carton color before they read a single label.

What cosmetic products fit in these boxes?

Compacts, palettes, lipstick (bullet and tube), lip balm, mascara, foundation, cream and serum jars, brushes, hair extensions, gift sets, and beauty subscription kits. Tell us your product dimensions and we size from there.

How do I decide between tuck, rigid, and mailer?

Tuck for retail compacts and lipstick under roughly $30 retail. Rigid for hero products, limited editions, and gift sets where the box adds perceived value. Mailer for D2C shipping and subscription boxes. A single brand line usually uses all three, with consistent print treatment so the family looks unified across formats.

Can you fit windows for shade visibility?

Window cutouts are available for makeup. We place the cutout to show the relevant part of the product (powder pan, lipstick bullet, serum color) and engineer the surrounding carton so the structure stays strong. Window placement also affects how the carton ships flat-pack, so we plan it at the dieline stage.

Do you handle INCI and free-from claim space?

Yes. Side and back panels are reserved for INCI ingredient lists, regulatory text, manufacturer information, and free-from claims (paraben-free, fragrance-free, vegan, cruelty-free). The front and top panels stay for brand and product imagery. We do not approve regulatory copy. That is on your team or your regulatory consultant. We make sure the layout fits everything legibly.
