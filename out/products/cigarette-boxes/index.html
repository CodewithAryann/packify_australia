Custom Cigarette & Vape Boxes | Compliance, Branding & Structure

Cigarette, vape, and cannabis-adjacent products carry the heaviest regulatory load in retail packaging: health warnings, ingredient panels, lab QR codes, batch numbers, child-resistant requirements. The carton has to fit all of it without burying the brand. Our cigarette and vape boxes are engineered around that constraint: clear panel zones for compliance copy, structures that support 16 CFR §1700-tested closures, and finishes that still let the box feel premium on a dispensary shelf.

Compliance-First Panel Layout

Front panel reserved for brand and product. Side and back panels carry warnings, ingredients, batch codes, and lab QR codes. Nothing crowds the face, nothing hides the regulatory text.

Child-Resistant Structure Support

Federal CR rules require third-party 16 CFR §1700.20 testing. We build cartons that pair with your tested CR closure (pre-tested press-and-turn slides, lock-and-tab inserts, or your existing CR partner system). We do not certify closures ourselves.

Tamper Evidence Built In

Perforated tear strips, shrink-wrap notches, and label-slot positions designed into the dieline so the tamper feature is part of the box, not a sticker added after.

Moisture & Light Barriers

Foil-lined or coated paperboard for cartridges and e-liquid bottles. Opaque board for THC products in states that require it. Inner sleeves for double-walled protection.

Tiny-Carton Information Hierarchy

Cartridge and pod boxes are physically small. We design panel hierarchies so strain, type, and potency stay legible while regulatory copy still fits cleanly without 4pt type.

Insert Systems for Leak Prevention

Custom foam or cardboard inserts hold cartridges upright and locked in place. Prevents the tipping that lets oil migrate to the mouthpiece during shipping or shelf restocks.

What 16 CFR §1700 Actually Requires (And What It Does Not)

Federal child-resistant rules sit under the Poison Prevention Packaging Act, enforced by the Consumer Product Safety Commission under 16 CFR §1700.15 and §1700.20. The standard is performance-based: 80% of children aged 42–51 months must fail to open the package within 10 minutes, and 90% of adults must open and reclose it correctly. Testing is done by third-party labs against ASTM D3475 protocols. The carton itself does not get tested. The closure system does. We build boxes that integrate with your tested CR closure, but the certification stays with the closure manufacturer or your testing partner.

State rules add a second layer. Every legal cannabis market requires CR packaging that meets the federal standard, but states diverge on opacity, plain-packaging restrictions, dual-symbol requirements, tamper-evidence, and labeling specifics. A Colorado-compliant carton may fail Massachusetts or New Jersey inspection. We build to your jurisdiction. Tell us where you sell and we work backwards from that state's rules.

Designing Tiny Cartons That Still Sell the Brand

A 0.5 ml or 1 ml cartridge box is roughly the size of a pack of gum. It still has to carry strain name, type, potency, batch, lab QR code, warnings, and brand identity. The trick is panel hierarchy: front for brand and primary product information, side panels for batch and potency, back for full regulatory copy and lab disclosures. Optional die-cut windows let buyers see oil color and fill level without the carton losing structural integrity.

Vape & Cannabis Categories We Build For

The format catalog below covers most Australian vape and cannabis-adjacent products.

  • Disposable vapes (single-use, 0.5 ml–5 ml)
  • Vape pen kits and starter kits
  • Vape cartridges (510-thread and proprietary, 0.5 ml–1 ml)

CR-Closure-Ready Cartons

Carton structures built to integrate with third-party-tested 16 CFR §1700 closures. We do not certify CR closures ourselves; we build the box that supports yours.

Premium Without Hiding the Compliance

Soft-touch, spot UV, foil stamping, and embossing on the brand zones. Compliance copy stays clean and legible alongside premium finishes.

Are these boxes child-resistant?

The carton itself is not what gets CR-tested under 16 CFR §1700.20. The closure system is. We design boxes that integrate with your tested press-and-turn slide, lock-and-tab insert, or other CR mechanism. If you need a CR partner, we can refer you. The certification stays with the closure manufacturer or your testing lab.

Do you handle state-specific cannabis packaging rules?

Yes. Tell us which states you sell in. We design to that state's rules: opacity requirements (Massachusetts, New Jersey), dual-symbol mandates, plain-packaging restrictions, child-resistance baseline. Multi-state operators usually need different cartons for different markets, and we plan the print runs accordingly.

Can you fit cartridge, pod, and disposable formats?

Yes. We size cartons to 0.5 ml, 1 ml, 2 ml, 3 ml, and 5 ml device formats: 510-thread, proprietary pods, Juul-compatible, and all common disposable shapes. Inserts hold devices upright to prevent leak migration during transport.

What about tamper evidence?

Three options, picked at the dieline stage: perforated tear strips that destroy on opening, shrink-wrap notches for an outer film seal, or label-position cutouts for a tamper-evident sticker that breaks across the closure. We build the feature into the carton, not added on later.
