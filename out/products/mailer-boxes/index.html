What Makes a Box a Mailer Box?

Mailer boxes are one-piece cartons with flaps that lock into place without tape. The structure is strong enough to ship as-is, with no outer shipping box needed. Corrugated fluting or heavy paperboard provides crush resistance. Sizes are customized to the product to minimize dimensional weight and reduce shipping cost.

We produce mailer boxes in white SBS, kraft, or corrugated. Single-wall corrugated for lighter items; double-wall for heavier or fragile products. Full-color printing on exterior and interior. Matte, gloss, or soft-touch lamination. No minimum order. Production 8–10 business days. Free shipping within Australia.

Interior Print as a Brand Asset

Mailer boxes are one of the few packaging formats where interior printing pays back reliably. Subscription, gift, and PR brands print branded artwork, thank-you messages, or customer onboarding flows on the inside surfaces, and customers photograph it. The cost premium over exterior-only is small relative to the social reach when unboxing content gets shared. Even if you do not push for unboxing content, the interior treatment changes how the box feels in the customer's hands and signals that the package was designed, not just commodity-shipped.

Right-Sizing for Dimensional Weight

Couriers (Australia Post, DHL, Aramex, TOLL) bill on whichever is greater: actual weight or dimensional weight (length × width × height ÷ a divisor). A box one size too big can double your shipping cost on the same product. We help you size mailers to your product to keep DIM weight as low as possible.

Single-Wall vs Double-Wall Decision

Single-wall corrugated handles items under ~2 lb in normal courier handling. Double-wall is for heavier products, fragile items, or anything that ships internationally and goes through more handling stages. Picking wrong on either side either wastes money or risks crush damage.

Interior Print as Brand Asset

Interior printing turns the unboxing reveal into a brand moment. Most photographed parts of a subscription unboxing on social media are interior surfaces. The cost premium over exterior-only is small relative to the marketing reach when customers post.

What can you ship in mailer boxes?

Apparel, shoes, cosmetics, electronics, books, subscription kits, promotional and PR mailers, and most D2C products that fit within typical dimensions. Items over ~2 lb usually need double-wall corrugated. Fragile items benefit from custom inserts (foam, paper, or molded pulp).

Can you print on the inside of mailer boxes?

Yes. Interior printing creates a branded reveal when the customer opens the box. Cost depends on print coverage and complexity. We quote exterior-only and full interior+exterior at the same time so you can compare. Brands posting unboxing content typically find the interior premium pays back fast.

Do mailer boxes need an outer shipping box?

No. Mailer boxes are designed to ship as-is. The structure and board weight are chosen to handle courier movement directly. Some brands add a poly outer for weather protection on long-haul or international shipments. Inside the box, void fill or inserts handle product protection if needed.

Are mailer boxes recyclable?

Yes. Cardboard, paperboard, and corrugated are all curbside-recyclable in most Australian municipalities. Kraft and recycled-content options carry the eco signal more visibly. Soy-based and water-based inks are available for brands building a verifiable sustainability story.
