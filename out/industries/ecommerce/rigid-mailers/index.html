Rigid Mailers: Self-Locking E-Commerce Boxes

Why Self-Locking Matters at the Bench

A packer assembling a taped box handles a tape gun, a roll, and a blade for every parcel, and that time compounds across a shift. A self-locking mailer folds shut by hand, removing the tape step entirely and the consumable cost with it. Packify builds the locking ears into the dieline rather than as an add-on, so the closure holds through the parcel network without a strip of tape anywhere on the box.

Choosing a Flute Grade

Corrugated is not one material. A thin flute keeps a light apparel parcel cheap and crisp for printing, while a heavier flute carries dense goods without the walls bowing under a stacked pallet. Packify specifies the grade against the shipped weight rather than defaulting to one board for everything, which is the difference between a parcel that survives a sortation belt and one that arrives dented.

The Interior Is Free Brand Space

Every mailer already has an inside surface, and most brands ship it blank. Printing the reverse of each panel costs nothing structurally and delivers the entire brand moment at the instant a customer lifts the lid. Packify prints interior and exterior in the same pass, which is why a mailer is the cheapest unboxing photograph a direct-to-consumer brand will ever buy.

Right-Sizing Against Dim Weight

Carriers price parcels on dimensional weight, so a box larger than its contents is billed for air. Cutting the mailer to the product trims that billable volume and cuts the void fill needed to stop the goods rattling. Packify builds the dieline to the actual product footprint, and with no minimum a brand can run several sizes across a catalogue rather than forcing everything into one box.
