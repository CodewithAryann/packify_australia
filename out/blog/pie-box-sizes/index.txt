Pie box dimensions track the pie's stated diameter directly. Unlike pizza boxes, which typically add 0.5 to 1 inch of clearance per side, most commercial pie boxes are labeled and sold at the same number as the pie: a 9-inch pie ships in a 9-inch box, not a 10-inch one.

A 2.5-inch depth is the industry default across all four diameters because it clears a standard pan wall (1 to 1.25 inches) plus enough headroom for a domed or lattice crust. Bakeries that assume every pie needs extra footprint clearance beyond its stated diameter end up carrying box inventory they do not actually need for most orders.
