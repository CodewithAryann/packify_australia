Halloween SKUs need a tighter palette than the rest of the fall line. Drop it to burnt amber and hold the motif to one element: a moth, a wax seal, a crescent. Board weight matters more than finish on tall vessels. Specify 16pt to 18pt SBS, roughly 350 to 400 GSM, for candle folding cartons. Candle shipping boxes do another job: a printed folding carton nests inside a corrugated shipper with 1 to 1.5 inches of crush space on all six sides, held off the walls by a die-cut insert.

Specify 32 ECT single wall corrugated cardboard for single candles under 3 lb, and 44 ECT or double wall for three-candle sets over 5 lb. Board grade follows candle vessel weight, not carton size. Die-cut corrugated or molded pulp inserts beat loose fill, which migrates during the fall carrier surge and leaves the vessel against a wall.

Right-sizing the shipper pays for the insert. Major carriers bill dimensional weight using a divisor of 139 for domestic parcels. A 12 oz carton at 3.75 x 3.75 x 4.625 inches shipped in a 9 x 9 x 9 box calculates to 729 divided by 139, which rounds up to 6 billable pounds. Drop to a 6 x 6 x 7 shipper, which still clears the carton by over an inch on every side, and it bills at 2 pounds.
